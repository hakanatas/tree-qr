Magic Tree

Every leaf on this tree is one module of a QR code. Tap the tree and the camera lifts overhead while the canopy settles into a scannable code for your link — tap again and it grows back.

  • Paste any link in the field below the tree.
  • Point a phone camera at the code to open it.
  • Switch seasons to recolour the canopy.

Built from scratch with three.js and a hand-rolled QR encoder — no tracking, nothing leaves your device.
